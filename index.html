Before you go

Good to know.

A few quick answers so you can plan your Berlin trip with confidence.

How is the estimated price split?

The example splits the €400 apartment total evenly among guests, then across three nights. With all six spaces filled, that’s €66.67 per guest total, or about €22.22 per night. Fewer guests means a higher share; see the table above. The host can adjust the total and nights.

Will I share a bed?

Some beds are shared. A double bed or sofa bed has two separately bookable sleeping spaces, so you may share with another PolyFest guest. The floor plan shows the bed types and availability.

What happens after I register interest?

Your application is an expression of interest only. When four people have applied, we’ll ask interested guests if they want to commit. If enough people confirm, we’ll check that the flat is still available and then make the booking. Timing and group size affect whether we can secure it.

How do later guests affect the price?

Once the group has committed and paid, if two more guests join, their contributions will be shared equally among the guests who already paid and returned to them as partial refunds. For example, if four guests have paid and two more join, the amount paid by those two new guests is divided between the original four. The host will confirm the final amounts before anyone commits.

What if the accommodation is cancelled?

We’ll let the group know as soon as we can and explain the accommodation payment arrangements. We’re sorry, but we can’t cover travel or other costs if the accommodation becomes unavailable or is cancelled for reasons outside our control. Please wait for the group commitment and booking confirmation before making non-refundable travel plans.

Can I ask about a stay without a ticket?

Of course. Send an inquiry and the host can answer questions. An inquiry does not reserve a sleeping space.
